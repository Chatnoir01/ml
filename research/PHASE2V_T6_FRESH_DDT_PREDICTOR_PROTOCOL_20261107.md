## Why a new protocol is necessary and how it differs from invalid T5

T5 initially required ZERO exact ciphertext block overlap between its pooled 15-SBox training pool and all test panels. A single cross-split 32-bit block collision in the last prespecified seed caused fail-closed invalidation, despite no established exact pair reuse. Such collisions are plausible when training 8,400 output blocks and testing 600–1,800 output blocks in an ~4.3-billion-element domain, particularly over many panels. **T6 does NOT remove, reseed, skip or reject a panel for any isolated single ciphertext-block coincidence across distinct independent S-Boxes/keys.** It PRESERVES full-data scores as the primary and reports every coinciding block and exact train/test pair overlap. A full **same ciphertext pair** in training and test is a hard scientific stop; no partial result may be promoted if that occurs. To test if block coincidences inflate accuracy, additionally calculate a **precommitted sensitivity AUC** excluding from each test panel all rows containing ANY ciphertext block also present in its corresponding training panel (same trained histogram, unchanged training records); report number excluded and the maximal abs/full-panel change. Do not use this sensitivity correction to select or retune a predictor.

## Prespecified source-only physical descriptor and prediction benchmark

- Original coarse descriptor `mean_max_DDT`: arithmetic mean across all 20 SBox of max of DDT entries for nonzero input difference.
- **Primary mechanism-aware descriptor**, from T5 *unchanged*: `mean_two_round_DDT_active_bytes` = average over the 20 boxes and input XOR differences (1 and 256) of expected number of active 8-bit bytes in the DIFFERENCE after TWO SBox rounds, assuming independent per-byte Markov DDT transitions and the exact original 11-bit left bit-rotation each round. First DDT row for input byte difference 1 enumerates outputs with `DDT[1,dy]/256`; rotate 11; in the second round compute per-output-byte probability zero as product of DDT probabilities for output masks from all active input byte differences; sum `1−P0` across 4 output bytes, average across first-round `dy`. This is an *approximation*, not an exact transition law for the fully keyed multi-round ToySPN.
- Primary target: evolution-group MEAN of four **unseen-SBox / R5** histogram AUC values (two diffs × two key arms). Secondary targets: same at R4 and within-group R4−R5.
- N=**12 independent new GROUP labels**, not 192 independent tests. Exactly 12 **leave-one-curriculum-group-out** folds per target and descriptor: (a) mean-of-other-11 baseline; (b) plain ordinary least squares with intercept + ONE feature on the other eleven groups, fallback mean only when feature has zero variance; no tuning or regularization, no sign reversal. Report all heldout predictions/slopes, AUC distributions, group feature ranges, and LOCO MAE/RMSE/relative SSE versus mean baseline.
- **Primary success criterion fixed before T6 outcomes**: DDT/rotation mechanistic descriptor R5 LOCO must outperform mean-only reference on BOTH MAE and SSE, without omitting or selecting any group. Otherwise hypothesis NOT supported. The coarse DDT scalar is a fixed comparison, not an excuse to choose a retrospective winner. No formal p-values (N=12 modest, family effects, data correlations).
