## Interpretation and limitations

Actual 16 original initialized checkpoint-zero model state SHA comparisons differed on **4/9 seeds**, while the first generation-5 fork is observed in **9/9**, including **5/9** exact checkpoint-zero model-state matching seeds. Initial A/F CPU/BLAS provenance is missing; it cannot be attributed to one backend or ruled out as a later confounder.

This provides original-source evidence for **different first-fork B1 ranks → different selected members → historically persistent descendants in some runs**. It **does not** establish that curriculum intervention caused the final fixed-arm heldout advantage, quantify a causal effect, demonstrate H1 forgetting, prove H2 cycling, or constitute an innovation in deployed cryptography. Later A/F populations and proposals are not exchangeable controlled comparisons. All nine seed groups and all null outcomes were reported. No p-values.
