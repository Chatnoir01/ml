# Phase 2V-T5 — prospective fresh-curriculum prediction with a DDT-aware two-round active-byte propagation feature
**Scientific registration 2026-11-07, committed BEFORE generation of T5 S-Boxes, datasets or T5 outcome AUCs.** This is a NEW synthetic research cohort. No neural models; no Phase 2U sealed neural fitting; original Phase 2G frozen verdict unchanged. DRAFT stacked on #174, no main merge.

## Primary question and original baseline failure
The retrospective Phase 2V-T4 analysis of 9 already-inspected historical curricula showed that predicting the five-round histogram distinguisher AUC from the curriculum average *maximum DDT entry* did not beat the mean-only predictor in leave-one-group-out analysis. Could a **mechanism-aware descriptor** computed from *full DDT transition rows and the exact 11-bit inter-byte rotation* predict five-round ciphertext-only distinguishability more reliably on a cohort **unseen when T4 was evaluated**?

This is a small-sample exploratory prospective falsification of one explicit weak predictor, NOT a claim of theoretical cryptographic security, a novel theorem, a cryptographic attack, or neural advantage.

Each seed creates 20 fresh 8×8 bijective S-Boxes via the original source algorithm `random.Random(evolution_seed); rng.shuffle(list(range(256)))`, rejecting duplicates. Exactly two original source input XOR differences **1 and 256**, exactly TWO **new independent 32-bit round-key schedules per S-Box** (arm names `K0`, `K1`), and only round counts **4,5** with the original bit-rotation **11** between rounds. Both R4 and R5 use the same panel plaintext pairs for matched comparisons, but never reuse these pairs across different S-Boxes or across different key arms.

For each (evolution seed, SHA3 fingerprint of S-Box, key arm K0/K1), derive **six** 32-bit public round-key words (enough for 5 rounds and final whitening) using `int.from_bytes(sha256(b"phase2v-t5-key-v1|" + str(evolution).encode() + b"|" + fp.encode() + b"|" + arm.encode() + b"|" + str(i).encode()).digest()[:4],"big")`, index i=0..5. Run the original pure-Python balanced pair generator exactly: 200 positive one-XOR-difference plaintext pairs and 200 negative non-equal and non-difference plaintext pairs, prevent any reuse of original plaintext blocks WITHIN each S-Box/key-arm/difference corpus, original shuffle, first 280 TRAIN, next 60 VALIDATION, last 60 TEST. **All independent domain seeds and keys must be logged by SHA256 evidence**. No same-class pattern reused across distinct SBoxes as occurred historically. Both R4 and R5 cipherize those same plaintext pairs with an independent *public* per-SBox six-word round-key schedule. Test labels not available during histogram fit. The 12 new sets must not be selected by reported AUC.

## Locked classical baseline and grouped output

One **per-(group,difference,key-arm,round)** four-position ciphertext XOR **naive-Bayes frequency histogram**, fit on ONLY the first 15 boxes' 4,200 train ciphertext-pair samples, Laplace smoothing alpha=1. Evaluate as fitted on 900 seen and 300 unseen test samples. Also fixed-score controls: constant AUC=0.5, `-popcount(C0 XOR C1)` and count of zero XOR output bytes. All AUC tie-aware with average-rank ties. No sign inversion, no hyperparameter search, no validation set tuning, no neural weights.

The PRIMARY target at evolution-group level is the **arithmetic mean of exactly four held-out-SBox R5 histogram AUC scores** (input differences 1,256 × key arms K0,K1). Secondary targets: mean four held-out R4 histogram AUCs and their within-group paired mean R4 minus R5. The **statistical sample size is N=12 groups**, not 192 independent observations. Each new group has a source SHA, full SBox/keys/plaintext evidence, and exact per-panel train/test ciphertext split SHA3.

## Precommitted predictors, derived WITHOUT test ciphertext labels

**Baseline 0**: training-eleven-groups target average (intercept-only), leave-one-group-out (LOCO).

**Existing comparison feature 1**: arithmetic MEAN over the group's 20 new S-Boxes of `max_{dx!=0,dy} DDT_S(dx,dy)`, computed from the full table.

**New mechanism-aware feature 2 (primary challenger)**: arithmetic MEAN over 20 S-Boxes and both original input differences of the **expected number of active BYTES in output XOR after TWO rounds**, under a single assumed Markov/independence transition driven by the FULL 8×8 SBox DDT and the exact 11-bit rotation:
- Begin with 32-bit difference `delta=1` or `delta=256`; each contains one nonzero byte. From the exact DDT row for that input byte difference, enumerate the first round's output byte difference `dy` with weight `DDT[input_dx,dy]/256`, apply exact rotate-left-11 and extract the four next-round 8-bit input differences.
- For each nonzero next-round byte difference, take its exact second-round SBox DDT output distribution. The second-round outputs of separate active bytes are treated as CONDITIONALLY independent in this *descriptor only*; outputs of zero-difference bytes are forced to zero. For each final output BYTE position j after rotating the second-round difference by 11, the probability of zero is the PRODUCT of the per-active-input-byte DDT probabilities that the relevant bits of its output difference are zero; the relevant input bit masks are EXACTLY derived by the 32-bit bit-permutation rotation. The expected number of active output bytes is `sum_j(1-P_j_zero)`, and average that expectation over first-round DDT transitions. This is a source-DTT/rotation mechanism feature, not a fit to any training labels.
- The descriptor depends on the actual input differences and round mixing, but assumes independent subbox transitions with a Markov approximation; it is **not** an exact 2-round cipher differential probability under fixed keys. The design cannot guarantee an association with 5-round ROC AUC. Its only predictive claim is tested by the locked LOCO below.

For each of the above two *scalar feature* models, fit **ordinary one-feature OLS + intercept** on the other 11 evolution groups and predict the held-out 12th. No regularization/tuning or extra feature combinations. If x variance is zero, fall back to intercept-only with recorded fallback. Compare **MAE, RMSE and 1 - SSE_feature/SSE_mean** against the same 12 mean-only fold outcomes. Report every actual/predicted group value, feature range, 12 learned slopes. Do NOT select whichever feature worked better after seeing the result as the predeclared hypothesis. No formal p-values/95% CI given small N, dependent families and the fact that feature design was motivated by previous inspected datasets.

**Primary hypothesis:** whether full-DDT/rotation active-byte feature 2 has **lower LOCO MAE AND lower LOCO SSE than mean-only** on the 12 genuinely new curricula's R5 held-out AUC. Hypothesis FAILED if either metric does not improve; no post-hoc threshold changes. Compare feature 1 for context; report both negative and positive results without changing endpoints.
